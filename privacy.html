U-TEAM Workspace — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

U-TEAM Workspace is a private tool used only by its owner to prepare research reports.

It accesses the owner's own Google account solely to read the owner's Google Sheets and Drive files and to create Gmail drafts. It never sends email automatically, never accesses other users' data, and does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to third parties.

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's private server and can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of Google user data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact: via the developer contact on the Google sign-in screen or github.com/ikakey.
